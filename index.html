About

I'm a Founding Member of Technical Staff at Exponential Security Labs, where I evaluate and red-team frontier AI models and build benchmarks and RL environments for AI security.

I studied Artificial Intelligence at the University of Amsterdam before joining the Max Planck Institute and ELLIS Institute Tübingen as a research intern, working with Maksym Andriushchenko on AI control and white-box monitoring.
